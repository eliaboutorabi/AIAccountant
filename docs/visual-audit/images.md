## Lower-priority refinements, not confirmed factual errors

- **M02 — `m02-decision-time.webp`:** the green arrows read visually as earlier history → agreed terms → prediction, although the lesson treats history and terms as separate available inputs. Two independent arrows entering prediction would be clearer. The information-time boundary and blocked future-data arrow are correct; this is a schematic-clarity refinement.
- **M03 — `m03-training-cycle.webp`:** a return arrow from the new prediction at step 4 points back toward step 1, which still displays the old USD 40 prediction. For a figure titled “One training update,” a simple 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 path would avoid suggesting the updated result resets. The training-cycle interpretation is defensible and all amounts/errors are correct.
- **M11 — `m11-text-to-vectors.webp`:** the leading space in the paid token is not drawn and two embedding rows omit their values. These are already explicitly explained by the current transcript, so they are not unresolved content defects. A visible whitespace marker would make the picture more self-contained.
- **M14 — `m14-evidence-bridge.webp`:** “−21 Costs” is intended as the cost contribution to profit, while the actual expense amount rose by 21. The current lead/transcript explain the sign correctly. “−21 Higher-cost impact” would reduce the need to consult the caption when the image is enlarged on its own.
- **M17/M20 — `m17-tool-boundary.webp`, `m20-failing-layer.webp`:** decorative calculator keys have repeated or abstract glyphs instead of a coherent numerical keypad. They do not encode the worked calculation, but blank keys would look cleaner if these images are edited for another reason.
- **M23 — `m23-draft-boundary.webp`:** tiny text on background receipts is pseudo-typeset and not reliably readable. The four instructional labels are sharp and correct. Replace the tiny receipt lettering with plain document lines if polishing the asset; no business claim depends on that lettering.
- **Original `agents.webp` and `language.webp`:** the toy robot and marble-brain metaphor are more juvenile than the newer professional artwork. This is a style opinion, not a rendering defect or a teaching-mechanism error. They are decorative chapter art, not explanatory diagrams.
